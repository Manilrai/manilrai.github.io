About Me

A bit about who I am

I'm Manil — a BCS (Hons) Computing student at Itahari International College, affiliated with London Metropolitan University, based in Itahari, Nepal. I genuinely enjoy building things for the browser. There's something satisfying about writing a bit of HTML, adding some CSS, throwing in JavaScript, and watching an idea come to life on screen.

Most of my hands-on experience comes from college group projects — things like building an internship portal with a skill gap analyzer, a travel and trekking platform, and a gym management desktop app. These taught me a lot about working with teammates, managing deadlines, and figuring out problems I hadn't seen before.

Right now, I'm actively looking for my first web development internship. I want to learn from experienced developers, contribute to real projects, and keep growing my skills. If you're looking for someone eager, reliable, and willing to learn — I'd love to connect.
